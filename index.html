03 / THE DECISION MODEL

Choose the right level of autonomy.

Tool count and workflow complexity are not the same as agentic behavior. Look for dynamic choices, goal-directed steps, and adaptation to intermediate results.

01

Deterministic workflow

Known steps, explicit rules, and predictable outcomes. Use conventional automation when it is sufficient.

Best when the path is known
02

LLM-enhanced workflow

A controlled process with targeted language understanding, extraction, classification, or generation.

Best when some steps need interpretation
03

Bounded agentic workflow

The system selects permitted next actions, observes results, and adapts its path toward a defined goal.

Best when the path must adapt
04

Agent with human approval

Dynamic investigation or recommendations with approval gates for consequential or irreversible actions.

Best when autonomy needs oversight
